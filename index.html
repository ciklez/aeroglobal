About us

From broker to asset-based operator

Close to two decades of building the fleet, the facilities and the know-how to move cargo on our own terms.

Who we are

Built in
Port Klang

Aero Global Logistics (M) Sdn Bhd was incorporated in 2008 as Traffic Shipping & Logistics (M) Sdn Bhd. In those early years the company worked purely as a logistics broker, outsourcing every movement to third-party providers.

That changed in 2013. A management takeover brought a new name and a new direction: rather than arranging transport, we would own and run it. Our haulage service started that same year with three prime movers and ten trailers.

The fleet has since grown tenfold, and our capabilities have grown with it, from reefer containers and side-loaders to a customs bonded haulage fleet and a dedicated Singapore to Port Klang lane.

In 2025 we founded Aero Global Logistics Solutions (M) Sdn Bhd to take the same hands-on approach beyond Malaysia, with a focus on international freight and consolidated forwarding.

01

Container Haulage

Container haulage to and from ports throughout Malaysia.

Haulage is where Aero Global began as an operator, and it is still the core of our business. We move import and export containers between ports, depots, factories and warehouses using our own fleet of 30 prime movers and 100 skeletal trailers, so the truck that collects your container is one we dispatch, maintain and answer for.

Our fleet handles more than standard dry containers. Gen-sets keep refrigerated containers powered on the road. Side-loaders place and lift containers at sites that have no handling equipment of their own. Our customs bonded haulage fleet moves dutiable cargo under bond.

From our base in Port Klang we serve ports throughout Malaysia, and we run a dedicated haulage lane between Singapore and Port Klang for cargo moving between the two.

  • 20ft and 40ft container haulage
  • Reefer containers with gen-set support
  • Side-loader delivery and collection
  • Customs bonded haulage
  • Singapore to Port Klang haulage
  • Empty container return to depot

Local Trucking

Domestic trucking for deliveries across Malaysia.

Not every load travels in a shipping container. Our local trucking service moves palletised, loose and mixed cargo between ports, warehouses, factories and customer sites across Malaysia.

Tell us the pickup point, the destination and the cargo, and we will match the right vehicle to the job, whether it is a one-off delivery, a regular distribution run or stock transfers between your own facilities.

Because trucking sits alongside our haulage, warehousing and customs teams, cargo that has just cleared the port or come out of storage can go straight onto the road without being handed over to another company.

  • Door-to-door domestic delivery
  • Full and part loads
  • Scheduled distribution runs
  • Port, warehouse and factory transfers
  • Last-mile delivery to customer sites
  • Coordinated with haulage and warehousing

Cross-Border Trucking

Overland freight between Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indo-China.

For many shipments within the region, road is faster and simpler than sea and more economical than air. Our cross-border trucking service carries cargo overland from Malaysia south to Singapore, north into Thailand and onward into Indo-China.

Border crossings are where overland freight usually loses time. We prepare export and transit documentation before the truck sets off, so the paperwork is ready at the checkpoint instead of being put together while your cargo waits.

With customs clearance handled in-house, a single booking can cover collection, export clearance, the border crossing and delivery at destination.

  • Malaysia to Singapore
  • Malaysia to Thailand
  • Onward routes into Indo-China
  • Export and transit documentation
  • Door-to-door across borders
  • Combined with customs clearance

Air Freight

International and domestic air freight for time-critical cargo.

When a shipment cannot wait, air freight closes the distance. We arrange international air cargo into and out of Malaysia, as well as domestic air freight within the country.

Through Aero Global Logistics Solutions we secure space with airlines, prepare air waybills and handle import and export clearance, with airport pickup and delivery arranged at the Malaysian end.

When timelines allow, we can consolidate smaller shipments to lower the cost. When they do not, we move urgent cargo on the next available flight.

  • International import and export
  • Domestic air freight
  • Consolidated and direct shipments
  • Urgent and time-critical cargo
  • Air waybill and documentation
  • Airport pickup and delivery

Sea Freight

International and domestic ocean freight, in full or shared containers.

Sea freight is still the most economical way to move volume, and being in Port Klang places us at one of the busiest container gateways in the region. We handle international shipments in both directions as well as domestic sea freight within Malaysia.

Ship a full container load when you have the volume, or use our consolidated less-than-container-load service to share space and pay only for what you use.

Because our group also runs the haulage, the containers travelling to and from the terminal move on our own trucks. That means one team following your cargo from origin to destination, with fewer handovers along the way.

  • Full container load (FCL)
  • Less than container load (LCL)
  • International import and export
  • Domestic sea freight
  • Consolidated forwarding

06

Warehousing

Customs bonded, cold room and transloading facilities.

We offer storage across a range of facilities, so your cargo is kept in conditions that suit it rather than placed wherever there happens to be space.

Customs bonded warehousing lets importers store goods with duties and taxes suspended until release, which eases cash flow for stock held ahead of demand. Cold rooms keep chilled and temperature-sensitive products within range. Transloading warehouses allow cargo to move quickly between containers, trucks and pallets without a long stay.

Our warehousing links directly with our haulage and trucking, so containers arrive from the port and orders leave for customers in one continuous flow.

  • Cold room storage

  • Container stuffing and unstuffing

07

Project Moves

Planning and transport for oversized and heavy cargo.

Some cargo will not fit on a standard trailer. Machinery, plant equipment, industrial vessels and structural components need a transport plan built around the load itself.

Every project move starts with an assessment of dimensions, weight and route. From there we arrange suitable equipment, lifting, permits and escorts where required, and schedule the move around site readiness and road restrictions.

Project cargo often involves several legs, such as sea freight into port, customs clearance and a final heavy-haul delivery to site. We coordinate the whole sequence so every handover is planned in advance, not improvised on the day.

  • Oversized and out-of-gauge cargo
  • Heavy machinery and plant equipment
  • Route surveys and planning
  • Crane lifting coordination
  • Permits and escorts where required
  • Multi-leg sea, port and road moves

08

Customs Clearance

Import and export clearance handled alongside your freight.

Clearance is often where shipments stall. Our customs team prepares and submits declarations for import, export and transhipment cargo with the Royal Malaysian Customs Department, so your goods can be released and moved without avoidable delay.

Correct tariff classification and complete paperwork are what keep cargo from being held for queries. We check commercial invoices, packing lists and supporting permits before submission, and raise anything missing early.

Clearance ties directly into our bonded haulage, bonded warehousing and freight services, so once cargo is released it can be collected, stored or delivered by the same team.

  • Import and export declarations
  • Transhipment and transit cargo
  • Tariff classification and duty assessment
  • Document checks before submission
  • Permit coordination where required
  • Linked to bonded haulage and warehousing
